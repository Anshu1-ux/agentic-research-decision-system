A phased migration strategy from a data warehouse to a lakehouse typically follows
the Medallion Architecture: Bronze (raw ingested data), Silver (cleaned and
conformed data), and Gold (business-level aggregates ready for BI tools).

Best practice is to run the lakehouse and legacy warehouse in parallel during
migration, validating that Gold-layer outputs match legacy warehouse reports
before cutting over any single team or dashboard. Attempting a full cutover in
one step is the most common cause of failed lakehouse migrations, since
undiscovered edge cases in legacy ETL logic often only surface once real users
depend on the new system.
