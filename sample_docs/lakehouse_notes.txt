Migrations from a traditional data warehouse to a lakehouse architecture almost
always go wrong when treated as a simple lift-and-shift. A data lakehouse combines
the low-cost, scalable storage of a data lake with the structure, ACID transactions,
and governance features of a data warehouse.

Organizations should generally avoid migrating to a lakehouse if they have a small,
stable data model, a team without Spark or distributed-systems experience, heavy
existing investment in warehouse-native features, or no serious machine learning
workloads planned.
